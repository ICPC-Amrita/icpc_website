SECTION 8 — HOW ICPC WORKS
From your first registration to the Regional
01 — FORM YOUR TEAM
Build an eligible team of three student contestants from your institution and identify your coach
according to the official ICPC eligibility rules.
↓
02 — REGISTER
Complete your ICPC registration for the Amritapuri preliminary round.
↓
03 — COMPETE ONLINE
Participate in the ICPC India Preliminary Online Round.
↓
04 — EARN YOUR ONSITE SPOT
Teams are evaluated according to the applicable ICPC selection process. Eligible
top-performing teams receive onsite invitations.
↓
05 — COMPETE AT THE REGIONAL
Experience the full ICPC Regional onsite with your team.
↓
06 — AIM HIGHER
Strong performances can lead toward the next stages of the ICPC pathway, including the Asia
West Championship and ultimately the World Finals, subject to official qualification rules.

SECTION 18 — FAQ
Questions teams ask before registering
Can beginners participate?
Absolutely. ICPC is open to eligible university students, and you do not need to be an expert
competitive programmer to participate.
How many students are there in a team?
An ICPC team consists of three contestants, subject to the official eligibility rules.
Does my team need a coach?
Follow the official ICPC team and coach requirements for your regional registration.
How much is registration?
The preliminary-round registration fee for Indian teams is ₹1,500 per team.
Is the preliminary round online?
Yes. The ICPC India Preliminary Round is conducted online.
How are teams selected for the onsite round?
Selection is based on performance in the preliminary round and the applicable ICPC regional
selection rules.
Where will the Amritapuri Regional be conducted?
The 2026 Amritapuri multisite regional is being conducted across four locations:
Kollam · Bengaluru · Coimbatore · Mysuru
Do I have to travel to Kerala?
Not necessarily. Amritapuri is a multisite regional with four announced locations.

IMPORTANT COPYWRITING PRINCIPLE
Do not repeatedly tell students:
"ICPC is prestigious."
Show them why they should care.
Instead of:
ICPC is the world's most prestigious programming competition.
Say:
Three students. One computer. Five hours. Solve together under pressure.
Instead of:
Improve your problem-solving skills.
Say:
Learn to break down problems that don't have obvious answers.
Instead of:
Great for your resume.
Say:
Build a competition record that demonstrates how you think, not just what
you have studied.
Instead of:
Beginners can participate.
Say:
You don't have to be an expert to start. Your first ICPC can be the beginning
of your competitive-programming journey.
Instead of:
We have four cities.
Say:
One Regional. Four Locations. Choose the place that works for your team.
The website should make the student feel the opportunity, not simply read about it.
